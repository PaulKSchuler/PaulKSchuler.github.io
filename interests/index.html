Tea network

Every node is an ingredient in one of the teas I drink, and two ingredients are linked when they appear in the same tea. Thicker links mean the pair turns up in more teas, bigger nodes are the more common ingredients, and colours mark groups of ingredients that tend to go together.

Drag the nodes to pull the network around. Hover over or tap an ingredient to see what it is combined with.
