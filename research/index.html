Research

My main research interests lie in collective attention and collective action, complexity science and social networks. In my PhD project, I analyse the interdependent roles of place of residence and social networks in the provision of social support to young adults in Switzerland. I like to explore new methods and interdisciplinary topics.

PhD project

The role of place and personal networks in the provision of social support

In this chapter of my PhD dissertation I discuss the importance of social and spatial data in the analysis of social support. I investigate the relative importance of social networks and place of residence with cross-classified multilevel models.

Based on my Master's thesis

Holey diamonds: structural holes in signed networks

This research project builds on my Master's thesis. We investigate what unobserved negative ties mean for the concept of structural holes.
