Research

I am a sociologist interested in social networks, social support and well-being, social capital and social cohesion, and the group identities and norms that hold networks together. I like to explore new methods and interdisciplinary topics, from multilevel models to agent-based simulation.

Since September 2025 I am a postdoctoral researcher at Coalesce Lab, Autonomous University of Barcelona, in the ERC-funded PATCHWORK project led by Miranda Lubbers. Before that I did my PhD in Sociology at the University of Edinburgh (2021–2026), funded by the Scottish Graduate School of Social Science, with a long affiliation to the MRC/CSO Social and Public Health Sciences Unit in Glasgow and the LIVES doctoral programme in Switzerland.

Current work

Know your network

How well do people know their own networks? With Michał Bojanowski and Miranda Lubbers I study how network structure and response bias shape estimates of personal networks and social capital. We presented the work at EUSN and ESA in 2026, and a related chapter on simulating an empirically informed population network of core discussion ties appeared in Computational Social Science of Social Cohesion and Polarization (Springer, 2026).

PhD, University of Edinburgh

The role of place and personal networks in the provision of social support

My dissertation asks how social networks and place of residence together shape the support young adults in Switzerland receive. Using the CH-x youth survey, I combine social and spatial data in cross-classified multilevel models to compare the relative importance of the two. Parts of this work were presented at Sunbelt, EUSN and ESA, and feed into chapters of the CH-x youth report 2025 and a forthcoming handbook chapter on family networks (with Gil Viry).

With Srebrenka Letina and Károly Takács

Holey diamonds: structural holes in signed networks

This project grew out of my Master's thesis at Linköping University. We ask what unobserved negative ties mean for the concept of structural holes and brokerage. The paper, Holey diamonds: Negative ties and open triads, is accepted for the Complex Networks 2026 proceedings.

  • Presentation EUSN 2022

With Rense Corten

Quantity over quality? Rating dynamics in online service markets

A research internship at Utrecht University turned into a longer project on how reputation builds up in online service markets, presented at INAS in 2022 and 2024.
